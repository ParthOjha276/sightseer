        How this verdict was made

        Each day is scored on five signals. The worst one becomes the sentence; the rest drop to the smaller tags. When several tie, this is the order that wins:

        Rain and thermal stress change whether you go out. Wind and sun change what you wear. The headline belongs to whichever changes the bigger decision.

        SignalWorth a mentionSerious

        Rain falling outside 9am–7pm is scored one level lower than the same rain during the day. A 90% chance at 3am is not your problem.

        The same numbers are then read hour by hour, inside whichever part of 9am–7pm is actually daylight, to work out which hours are worth being outside. Only rain and temperature can close an hour, for the reason above: wind and sun change what you wear, not whether you go.
